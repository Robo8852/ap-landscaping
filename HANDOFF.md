## 3. Post-launch queue (within days of flipping noindex)

### 3a. Google Search Console

Verify domain ownership (DNS TXT or HTML file). Submit `https://ayclandscaping.com/sitemap.xml`. Track when Google starts indexing and what queries the site appears for.

### 3b. Google Business Profile

**For a local landscaping business, GBP matters more than organic indexing.** Local-pack ("3-pack" map results) is where "landscapers near me" searches surface. Requires:

- Verifiable business address (postcard verification typically — takes 5–10 days to arrive)
- Owner needs to claim the listing under `ayclandscaping@gmail.com`
- Once verified, fill out: hours, service area, services list, photos, description

This is mostly client work but blocks local discoverability.

### 3c. Analytics

Without analytics you launch blind. Pick one:

- **Plausible** (~$9/mo) — paid, hosted, privacy-friendly, no cookie banner
- **Umami** (free if self-hosted, $20/mo hosted) — same shape
- **Vercel Web Analytics** (free up to limits, already in the project's reach)

Wire in `<Analytics />` component or `<Script>` per provider docs.
